Department Responsibilities

Each team has a specific focus, working together to create a seamless digital ministry experience.

💻 Audio/Visual (AV)

  • Manage live sound, video projection, and lighting during services.
  • Operate cameras and streaming software for online broadcasts.
  • Record sermons and events for later use by the Content team.
  • Ensure a high-quality, distraction-free technical experience.

🎨 Content Creation

  • Film, edit, and produce videos (sermon clips, testimonies, event promos).
  • Design graphics for social media, sermon series, and events.
  • Collaborate with the AV team to repurpose recorded content.
  • Ensure all content aligns with the church's message and brand.

📢 Social Media

  • Post approved content across all church social media platforms.
  • Engage with the online community through comments and messages.
  • Create and manage a content calendar to ensure consistent posting.
  • Monitor trends and analytics to increase reach and engagement.

🤝 Follow-Up

  • Monitor all platforms for prayer requests and questions about faith.
  • Respond to initial inquiries and connect individuals with regional leaders.
  • Track all online requests for Bible studies and report them weekly.
  • Ensure every person who reaches out feels seen, heard, and valued.

Defining Our Roles: Congregational vs. Regional

Clarity in our roles ensures we function as one body. Congregational leadership steers the ship, while regional teams power the engine room.

Congregational Responsibilities

  • Vision & Strategy: Work with church leadership to set the overall direction, goals, and brand for the Cyber Ministry.
  • Major Events: Lead the planning and execution for large-scale events like conferences and Tri-State services.
  • Training & Resources: Equip regional teams with the skills, tools, and best practices they need to succeed.
  • Content Approval: Work with church leadership to ensure all major content is theologically sound and aligns with our brand.

Regional Responsibilities

  • Weekly Execution: Manage all technical aspects (AV, streaming) for local weekly services and events.
  • Local Content Capture: Record sermons, capture photos, and gather testimonies from the local congregation.
  • Community Engagement: Manage local social media presence and perform initial follow-up with contacts from your region.
  • Team Coordination: Organize and schedule local team members to ensure all roles are covered for services.
  • Reporting: Communicate needs, successes, and online engagement metrics back to congregational leadership.
